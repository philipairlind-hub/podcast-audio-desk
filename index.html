Do you edit video too?

This offer is for audio only. Video, shorts, subtitles and publishing are not included.

How many edits or revisions?

Basic removal of clearly marked mistakes and long pauses plus one round of minor corrections. Full transcript-based storytelling or heavy editorial restructuring requires a separate quote.

Can you make a terrible recording sound perfect?

No. Some problems cannot be fixed cleanly. I review a short sample and decline or rescope unsuitable material before charging.

Is the monthly plan an automatic recurring charge?

No. Monthly production is currently an individually agreed service, not an active auto-renewing subscription. Any future subscription arrangement would be separately disclosed and agreed to.

Do I need to book a call?

No. Written email and file handoff are sufficient for most straightforward audio-only projects.
