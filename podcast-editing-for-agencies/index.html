How an agency handoff works

  1. Written scope: share raw duration, number of separate voice tracks, delivery format, release cadence and cut-notes. Confirm confidentiality and asset rights.
  2. Paid sample: one funded first episode establishes sound reference, labour time and revision expectations before a recurring agreement.
  3. Repeatable delivery: deliver edited WAV/MP3, check opening/closing and transitions, return one consistent revision round.

What is deliberately outside scope?

No video, social clips, captions, transcription, guest booking, publishing, music licensing, live recording, content strategy or unlimited revisions. Badly clipped audio, extensive repair and longer/multitrack sessions need a separate quote.
